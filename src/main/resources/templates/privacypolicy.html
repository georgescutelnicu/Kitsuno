Privacy Policy & Terms

Last Updated: October 2026 · Kitsuno (kitsuno.help)

At Kitsuno, accessible from kitsuno.help, the privacy of our users is our top priority. This document outlines how we collect, store, and protect your information when using our platform.

1. Consent

By accessing or using Kitsuno, you agree to the terms described in this policy. This applies to all visitors and registered users within the Kitsuno ecosystem.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your username and email address. We store passwords using secure cryptographic hashing and never keep plain-text passwords.
  • Automatic API Key Assignment: Upon creating an account, a developer API key is automatically generated and linked to your profile to authenticate REST requests.
  • Flashcards & Learning Notes: When you save kanji flashcards, attach custom vocabulary, or add personal notes, this data is saved to your account so it stays synchronized across your devices.
  • Communications: If you reach out to us via email, we keep the contents of your inquiry to help resolve support issues.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Kitsuno uses essential cookies and local browser storage to keep you logged in across pages and maintain your preferences. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising or commercial tracking.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:

  • Deliver and maintain our core features (flashcard deck management, API endpoints, grammar, and vocabulary lookups).
  • Authenticate your session and secure your account against unauthorized access.
  • Respond to direct customer service requests and account inquiries.

5. Infrastructure & Analytics Logs

Like most modern web services, standard non-identifiable system data (such as anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps) is handled at the server level solely to keep the service stable, secure, and protected against automated attacks.

6. Data Rights & Account Deletion

You own your personal data. If you wish to request a copy of your stored information or permanently delete your account and all associated saved flashcards, please contact us at kitsunojapanese@aol.com. We will process your deletion request manually within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Basic Terms of Service & Disclaimer

Kitsuno is provided free of charge to help you learn Japanese. While we work to keep everything accurate and running smoothly, we cannot guarantee that the site will always be error-free or uninterrupted. By using Kitsuno, you agree to use our platform and REST API responsibly. We reserve the right to suspend accounts that attempt to abuse automated endpoints or exploit the web application.
