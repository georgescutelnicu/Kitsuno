About Kitsuno

Learn Japanese, one character at a time

Kitsuno was built as a clean, simple tool for learning Japanese. Instead of forcing rigid routines or overwhelming you with ads, it puts all the essentials in one place: kana charts, kanji by grade, grammar guides, and flashcard tools you can actually customize and export.

What's Included

  • Kana Practice: Charts with audio pronunciation for Hiragana and Katakana, plus a practice mode to test yourself.
  • Kanji: Browse kanji by grade with animated stroke orders, readings, meanings, and common vocabulary.
  • Grammar & Sentences: Follow short grammar lessons step by step, or search words to see example sentences in context.
  • Flashcards: Create custom flashcards from any kanji page, add your own notes, and export your collection to Anki, CSV, or JSON.
  • REST API: Access Japanese character and kana data directly using your profile API key.

Resources & Attributions

Kitsuno relies on the following data sources and tools:

  • Kanji Data & Stroke Order: Character information and stroke diagrams provided by KanjiVG.
  • Grammar Lessons: Grammar guides and rules adapted from Yoku.bi.
  • Kana Details: Reference information gathered from Wikibooks (Hiragana / Katakana) and Wikimedia (Hiragana / Katakana).
  • Kana Audio: Audio recordings gathered from Tofugu.
  • Vocabulary & Example Sentences: Example sentences retrieved using the Jotoba API.
  • Icons & Graphics: Navbar icons from Lineicons and vocabulary section illustration by catalyststuff on Freepik.
